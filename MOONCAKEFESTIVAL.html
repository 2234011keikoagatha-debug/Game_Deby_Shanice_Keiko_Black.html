The Festival Between the Moon and Us

A celebration of the full moon, family reunion, Chinese culture, and the timeless legend of Chang'e and Houyi.

What is Mooncake Festival?

A festival celebrated under the brightest moon

🌕 The Mid-Autumn Festival

The Mooncake Festival, also known as the Mid-Autumn Festival, is a traditional Chinese festival celebrated on the 15th day of the eighth month of the lunar calendar.


The full moon symbolizes reunion and togetherness. Families traditionally gather, admire the moon, share mooncakes, and enjoy meaningful moments together.

The Legend of Chang'e

A story passed down through generations

🏹

Houyi, the Archer

Long ago, ten suns appeared in the sky, causing great suffering on Earth. Houyi, a legendary archer, shot down nine of them and saved the people.

💊

The Elixir

As a reward for his heroic act, Houyi received an elixir that could grant immortality. He kept it safely because he did not want to leave Chang'e.

🌙

Chang'e and the Moon

One day, Chang'e drank the elixir and floated toward the moon. She eventually became closely associated with the moon.

🥮

Remembering Chang'e

During the festival, people admire the full moon and enjoy mooncakes while celebrating traditions connected with this ancient legend.
